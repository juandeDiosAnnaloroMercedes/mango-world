# Decision log — MANGO WORLD

## Confirmed project choices
- Project name: MANGO WORLD.
- Separate from the original Mango project; do not modify the original website or repository.
- Blockchain direction: Ethereum.
- Wallet direction: non-custodial connection to a wallet controlled by the user (e.g. MetaMask); the application must not collect seed phrases/private keys.
- Proposed token ticker/name: MANGO / MANGO Coin.
- Maximum supply proposal selected by user: 100,000,000 tokens.

## Not yet decided / not authorised
- Mainnet deployment.
- Token distribution and initial recipient (the contract requires an explicit nonzero `INITIAL_HOLDER`; no recipient has been approved).
- Token utility, transfer restrictions, liquidity, exchange listing, EUR conversion or price claims.
- Public sale, airdrop, staking, rewards, custody or exchange services.
- Legal classification and any MiCA / Italian regulatory obligations.
- Trademark filing and legal protection.

## Design guardrails
- No guaranteed price, profit, liquidity or listing claims.
- No use of the original Mango identity or assets in a way that merges the projects without a separate decision.
- No mainnet deployment or real-money movement without a separate, explicit approval after legal and security review.
